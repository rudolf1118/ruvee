# Risk register

Status: **Proposed (Phase 0).** These are residual risks of the proposed design. They are not
accepted until the owner reviews them. The threats themselves are in
[threat-model.md](threat-model.md); this register tracks what remains after the design's
mitigations, and who has to act.

Likelihood and impact are rough estimates for a self-hosted, single-user deployment in private
mode: **H** high, **M** medium, **L** low.

## Changes after architecture validation

Status remains **Proposed**. Changes made on 2026-10-22 from
[architecture-validation.md](architecture-validation.md): R-02 (compiled `opaque-ke` version and
missing KSF vectors), R-03 (single-machine measurements), R-08 (junk-wrap case, decision D-22),
R-13 (host snapshots, no pin lockout), R-14 (exact WASM requirement verified). R-17 and R-18 are
new.

## Changes after the primitive validation harness (P2-01)

Status remains **Proposed**. Changes made on 2026-10-22: R-02 now cites the
[P2-01 harness](../../tools/crypto-validation/README.md) as the continuous library check, and
records V-15, an unreleased libsodium.js fix. R-03's owner action is D-26, which now blocks the
calibration task (P2-02).

## Changes after the Argon2id calibration (P2-02, local part)

Status remains **Proposed**. Changes made on 2026-10-23: R-03 adds the local benchmark results
from [kdf-calibration.md](kdf-calibration.md). Its likelihood and impact are unchanged, because
the reference devices are still unmeasured.
